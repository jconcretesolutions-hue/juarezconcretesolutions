HOW TO PUBLISH WITH NETLIFY
1. Unzip this folder.
2. Sign in to Netlify.
3. Drag the entire unzipped jcs_pro_website folder into Netlify Drop.
4. Netlify will publish the site and activate the estimate form.
5. In Netlify, open Forms to view estimate requests and configure email notifications.
6. Connect your purchased domain in Domain Management.
7. Replace YOUR-DOMAIN.com in robots.txt and sitemap.xml with the final domain.
